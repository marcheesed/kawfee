Privacy Policy


When an account is deleted, all data associated with the account is as well, and is therefore unrecoverable by our team. You acknowledge this upon deletion of an account. Do not reach out to our team for assistance in such.


What We Collect From Users

1. Any text, images, video or other forms of media that you upload to the site.

2. Any referral information, such as where you came from and where you go afterwards.

3. The countries in which the SSL request has come from.

4. Your web browser information.

5. Your device information.

6. What you do on the site, such as the URLs you visit.

7. Any information voluntarily given to us, via your browser, device or content uploaded.

8. Your IP address.


Usage of Collected User Information

User data is never sold, given to or accessed by third parties.

We may use your data to further improve and optimize our site, along with making the site a safer place, along with general information and statistics about the site and it's visitors.


Removal of Data

Upon deletion of an account, all user data collected on the site will be permanently erased. Please keep in mind this does not include ip logs, as those are used to continue to make the site a safer place, along with our statistics.
